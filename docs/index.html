Why TharMesh

Built for the gaps in coverage.

A village meetup, an outdoor group, or a local outage can leave people close by without a dependable internet connection. TharMesh explores how nearby phones can keep a conversation moving.

◉

Nearby connections

Android devices discover and connect over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi through Google Nearby Connections.

↗

Multi-hop relay

Participating phones can forward eligible messages across supported mesh paths when a direct connection is unavailable.

◷

Store and forward

Queued messages can retry as peers come within range. Expiry and hop limits apply.

How it works

From online setup
to offline chat.

In the account-enabled build, Supabase handles online account verification. Nearby devices carry mesh messages.

  1. Verify your account online. Use a supported email or Google sign-in option when configured.
  2. Activate your device. Set up or recover your messaging identity.
  3. Find nearby users. Grant the Android permissions the app requests for discovery.
  4. Exchange messages. Connected phones carry messages over nearby radio links.
  5. Use supported relay paths. Participating phones may forward messages when a path becomes available.

Security & privacy

Separate paths for accounts and messages.

Verify the person you add

Device keys support signed mesh messages. Compare contact QR information in person before relying on a contact’s identity.

Recover an identity when configured

The account-enabled preview includes encrypted recovery. Set it up and keep your recovery secret safe. The cloud account service is separate from the mesh transport.

Before you try it

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet?

The account-enabled preview needs internet for signup, sign-in and cloud account functions. Supported nearby messaging can operate offline after activation.

How far can a message travel?

There is no fixed coverage area. Range, available peers, obstacles, battery settings, permissions and Android restrictions affect delivery. Relays require participating devices and a viable path.

Can I rely on it in an emergency?

No. TharMesh is experimental. Messages may be delayed or never arrive. Use official emergency services and established communications channels.

What is available today?

The Android code includes nearby transport, relay and retry logic, and QR contact verification. Supabase authentication and encrypted identity recovery are in the account-enabled preview. Production sign-in, unattended device recovery, and real-world device compatibility are still being validated.
