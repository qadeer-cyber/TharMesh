Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy covers the public site and the account-enabled Android preview. Older offline-only builds may behave differently.

Who operates TharMesh

TharMesh is maintained by Abdul Qadeer Nohri. Contact the project maintainer for privacy inquiries. The support tracker is public. Ask for a private contact channel before sharing account details.

Online accounts

When enabled, Supabase Auth processes your email address, verification or sign-in credentials, unique account ID and session data. Google sign-in shares an identity token with Supabase to verify your identity. That token may contain your Google account identifier, email and basic profile claims. TharMesh uses Google account data for authentication and associated account identification. The app does not request access to Gmail, Drive or Google Contacts. Email verification may involve a configured email delivery provider.

Profiles, devices and recovery

Account features may store a handle, display name and optional profile image URL. Directory profile fields may be visible to other authenticated users. Device enrollment may store device identifiers, public keys and certificates. If you choose to upload a recovery backup, Supabase may store encrypted recovery data and metadata such as the account ID, encryption parameters and timestamps. Keep the recovery secret separately.

Messages and contacts

Messages, contact records and delivery state are stored on your device. Nearby participating phones can receive or temporarily hold and relay mesh data. Supabase is used for account functions and optional recovery, not as a message relay. Recipients can keep copies of what you send.

Nearby discovery and permissions

The app uses Google Nearby Connections over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Depending on your Android version, the app may request Nearby Devices or Location permissions and require related settings to be enabled for discovery. QR scanning may request camera access. Nearby devices can exchange discovery and routing information needed to connect and deliver messages. Google Play services may process information under Google’s privacy policy.

Service logs and website hosting

Cloud account requests may produce IP addresses, timestamps, request details and security or error logs at Supabase or its infrastructure providers. The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process standard request data under GitHub’s privacy statement. The site includes no advertising or analytics scripts.

Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until removed through available app or Android controls. Clearing app storage can permanently remove keys, contacts and conversations. Uninstalling or signing out does not delete server records, provider logs or copies on other devices. Contact the maintainer to request access, correction or deletion of cloud account data. Handling depends on verification, applicable law, available controls and provider retention.

Security and limitations

Account requests use HTTPS. Mesh signing keys and QR verification help authenticate supported messages and contacts. Recovery backups are encrypted before upload. These protections cannot prevent all risks from compromised devices, lost recovery secrets, incorrect verification or experimental software.

Children and policy changes

TharMesh is not directed at children under 13. Contact the maintainer if a child has supplied account information. We will publish material policy changes on this page and update the effective date. Account-enabled builds should display the current policy before public release.
